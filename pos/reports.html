Reports

Covers Sales, Payment Mode, Product/Category/Brand Sales, Discount, Customer, Purchase, Supplier, Sales/Purchase Returns, Stock Valuation, Low Stock, Out of Stock, Expiry, GST Summary by HSN, and an approximate Profit/Margin. Not yet covered: Expense Report and Cash Book (no expense-entry feature exists yet), and a filed-return- grade GST report (this one is a bookkeeping summary, not a filing tool) — see README.md section 10.

Choose a report and date range, then Run Report.
